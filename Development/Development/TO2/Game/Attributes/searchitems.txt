/************************************************************************
**
** SearchSet
**
** The SearchSet definitions define all of the attributes associated with
** a set of items that might be found while searching. 
**
**
** All SearchItem definitions should contain the following properties:
**
**  Name (String)
**
**      The user friendly name of the search set 
**
**  Items (String)
**
**		A comma-separated list of SearchItems found in this set
**
**	ItemPercent (Integer)
**
**		The percent chance that one of the SearchItems specified above will be found.  
**
**		NOTE: this must be a number between 0 and 100 so that ItemPercent + AmmoPercent +
**			  WeaponPercent + GearPercent add up to 100!
**
**	Ammo# (String)
**
**		Specifies the type of ammo that may be found and the ammount of ammo.
**		The value of this property (before the comma) MUST correspond to the 
**		name specified in the AMMO definitions in weapons.txt.  Multiple ammo 
**		types may be specified but only one of the choices will be randomly 
**		select.  For example:
**
**			Ammo0	= "9mm fmj, 10"
**			Ammo1	= "9mm dum dum, 30"
**			...
**
**	AmmoPercent	(Integer)
**
**		The percent chance that one of the ammo types specified above will be found.  
**
**		NOTE: this must be a number between 0 and 100 so that ItemPercent + AmmoPercent +
**			  WeaponPercent + GearPercent add up to 100!
**
**	Weapon# (String)
**
**		Specifies the type of weapon that may be found.  The value
**		of this property MUST correspond to the name specified in the
**		WEAPON definitions in weapons.txt.  Multiple weapons may be specified
**		but only one of the choices will be randomly select.  For example:
**
**			Weapon0	= "Grenade"
**			Weapon1	= "StunGrenade"
**			...
**
**	WeaponPercent (Integer)
**
**		The percent chance that one of the weapon types specified above will be found.  
**
**		NOTE: this must be a number between 0 and 100 so that ItemPercent + AmmoPercent +
**			  WeaponPercent + GearPercent add up to 100!
**
**	Gear# (String)
**
**		Specifies the type of gear that may be found.  The value
**		of this property MUST correspond to the name specified in the
**		GEAR definitions in weapons.txt.  Multiple gear items may be specified
**		but only one of the choices will be randomly select.  For example:
**
**			Gear0	= "Body Armor"
**			Gear1	= "Health"
**			...
**
**	GearPercent	(Integer)
**
**		The percent chance that one of the gear items specified above will be found.  
**
**		NOTE: this must be a number between 0 and 100 so that ItemPercent + AmmoPercent +
**			  WeaponPercent + GearPercent add up to 100!
**
**
*/
